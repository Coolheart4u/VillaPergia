Before you book

House rules

Check in

2:00pm to 8:00pm. Please let us know your arrival time in advance.

Check out

8:00am to 11:00am. Later check out is sometimes possible, subject to availability.

Payment

Payment before arrival by bank transfer. We will send instructions once your dates are confirmed. The balance is paid on arrival.

Security deposit

€100 on arrival, refunded in full at check out subject to an inspection of the villa.

Children

Children of all ages are welcome and there is no minimum age for check in. One crib is available on request, free of charge, subject to availability.

Cancellation

Terms depend on the season and the dates booked. Send us your dates and we will set out exactly what applies.

Smoking

The villa is entirely non smoking, indoors and out.

Pets

We are not able to accept pets.
