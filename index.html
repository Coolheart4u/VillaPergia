The villa

Designed to be a sanctuary

Villa Pergia sits in the quiet coastal village of Latchi, on the edge of the Akamas peninsula. The beach is a two minute walk. Latchi harbour and the Baths of Aphrodite are both a five minute drive, and the Blue Lagoon is a morning boat trip away.

A nine metre private pool

Yours alone, with loungers, a shaded pergola and a barbecue on the terrace beside it.

Open plan kitchen and dining

Dishwasher, washing machine, oven and hob, with a table for six and a breakfast bar.

Three bedrooms, sleeping seven

One double and two twins. The two upstairs rooms are en suite, both opening onto balconies.

The beach, fifty metres away

Quiet and unspoilt. Loggerhead turtles nest along this stretch of coast in summer.
