The area

What to do while you are here

Villa Pergia sits at the western end of Cyprus, where the Akamas peninsula begins and the coast road runs out. The Baths of Aphrodite are a five minute drive, Latchi harbour about the same, Paphos town half an hour, and the cedar forest around Kykkos Monastery forty five minutes inland.
